How it works

From tile to decision

Data moves one way through three stages. Each is useful on its own; together they close the loop without a human in it.

Stage 1 · In the fabric

Agents measure the real thing

Soft-IP agents drop into unused fabric across the die and report path margin, delay drift, junction temperature and rail droop. Parametric values, per region, at operating speed — not a model.

Stage 2 · In hardware

Thresholds fire before a path does

An on-die decision block turns threshold crossings into interrupts your firmware can catch in microseconds — long enough to back the clock off, raise the rail, or move the workload.

Stage 3 · In software

Applications act on it

The same stream feeds the tester, your fleet dashboard, and the next place-and-route run. Where the data lands is your choice: our cloud, your VPC, or fully on-prem.

Industries

Same fabric, different stakes

What a dropped path costs depends on where the board is. Pick your world.

Data center

Racks of accelerator cards, and no way to tell which one is about to slow down.

  • Per-card margin trend, so a degrading part is a maintenance ticket instead of an outage
  • Core rail scaling against real margin — the single biggest lever on rack power
  • Thermal maps per device, not per chassis inlet
Explore data center

AI and HPC

Training jobs run for weeks. A dropped path at hour 300 is expensive in a way nothing else is.

  • Margin watch during long jobs, with interrupts your runtime can catch
  • Per-region hot spot detection under sustained load
  • Headroom recovery on the parts that hold up best
Explore ai and hpc

Aerospace and defense

Long programs, hard environments, and a requirement to prove what happened.

  • Radiation and ageing effects tracked as a margin trend over the life of the unit
  • Bitstream authenticity checked on every boot, with a signed log
  • Data stays in your enclave — the analytics run on-prem
Explore aerospace and defense

Automotive

The part has to work at −40 °C and after fifteen years of thermal cycling.

  • Ageing measured directly rather than modelled from a mission profile
  • Diagnostics that map to your existing safety mechanism inventory
  • Screening at end-of-line that catches the parts a vector set misses
Explore automotive

Networking and 5G

Line cards in cabinets nobody wants to visit twice.

  • Predictive fault detection so the truck roll is planned, not emergency
  • Power reduction across a fixed thermal envelope
  • Remote margin readout without a debug probe on site
Explore networking and 5g

Test and measurement

You already own the tester. It just cannot see inside the fabric.

  • Margin readout as a test insertion, in the time budget you already have
  • Outlier detection on parametric data instead of pass/fail bins
  • Correlation from final test to field return, closed
