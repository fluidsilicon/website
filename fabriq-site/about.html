Home/About

We believe silicon should adapt, evolve, and heal.

The Semiconductor Industry Association predicts that computing electricity demand will exceed global supply by 2040. Fluid Silicon builds the critical infrastructure to advance computing capabilities without a proportional environmental impact.

The argument

The limits of conservative design

Highly-scaled technology nodes operating in hostile conditions like deep space or defense applications are susceptible to accelerated aging mechanisms like NBTI and Electromigration. Currently, chips fall out of specification without prior warning, leading to irrecoverable errors where replacement is physically impossible.

To prevent this, the delay model used in commercial chips is inherently conservative, guarding against the worst-case scenario. This assumes that chips in the same bin have identical characteristics, leaving massive performance and energy penalties on the table.

What we build

We build closed-loop self-healing systems. We deploy on-chip and online monitoring of basic elements of logic at the speed of operation. By coupling pico-second level timing sensors with robust on-chip control policies, we enable adaptive compensation and on-the-fly intra-CLB repair.

We reclaim the 30%+ vendor guard-band so your chips run faster, use less power, and survive longer.

How we work

Three core capabilities

01

Pico-second Precision

We configure timing sensors with pico-second (trillionth-of-a-second) precision using spare Configurable Logic Blocks to monitor silicon health at the speed of operation.

02

Under 1% Overhead

Our time-multiplexed architecture shares sensors amongst multiple CLBs. A single reserved block measures itself and 19 others, resulting in a 200x+ area overhead reduction.

03

True Self-Healing

Through robust on-chip control policies, our systems track changes over time, anticipate potential failures, and compute repair alternatives on-the-fly in safe or idle periods.
