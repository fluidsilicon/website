FPGA Engineer

careers@fluidsilicon.com · Subject: "FPGA Engineer"

About Fluid Silicon

Fluid Silicon builds infrastructure for the next generation of reconfigurable systems, to advance evolvable, and adaptive computing.

The Role

Fluid Silicon is fundamentally a research and development company that solve very hard problems, of which some of them have no clear solutions. We are loooking for passionate engineers who will use their creativity to build scalable systems and solutions to power the digital world.

As a founding engineer, opportunities for ownership and collaboration with leading experts in the field in both academia and the industry.

Responsibilities

  • Architect, implement and verify FPGA designs at the RTL level.
  • Build reconfiguration and repair flows that operate on live, running systems
  • Run silicon characterization campaigns across variation, aging, and different opearating points
  • Design firmware and software interfaces for FPGA platforms

Minimum Qualifications

  • BS or MS in electrical or computer engineering, or equivalent experience
  • 3+ years designing and verifying production RTL in SystemVerilog or VHDL
  • Deep working knowledge of AMD (Xilinx) or Altera device architecture and toolchains
  • Fluency in static timing analysis
  • Tcl and Python for flow automation

Preferred Qualifications

  • Experience with dynamic or runtime reconfiguration on shipped systems
  • Low-level bitstream or device database work, including open-source FPGA tooling
  • Research background in reliability, aging, or process variation
  • Publications or open-source contributions in reconfigurable computing

Compensation & Benefits

  • Competitive salary and equity
