Five capabilities, one platform

Everything below reads and writes the same thing: the measured health of the fabric, one BEL at a time.

01 · Monitor

BEL-level monitoring

Health sensing at every basic element of the fabric — LUTs, flip-flops, carry chains, routing.

  • Total coverage: every BEL instrumented, not a sampled subset
  • Continuous health state per resource while the design runs
  • The foundation every other capability builds on

02 · Query

In-silicon timing queries

Ask the running device for timing. It answers with measurement, not a model.

  • Path delay read directly from the silicon, at operating voltage and temperature
  • Per-resource resolution: know which BEL is slow, not just which path
  • On demand or scheduled — the query engine runs alongside the design

03 · Adapt

Adaptive compensation

When a resource degrades, the platform responds instead of the design failing.

  • Logic remapped to healthier fabric resources, transparently to the application
  • DVFS: voltage and frequency scaled against measured timing to hold the spec
  • Degradation becomes a managed event, not a fault

04 · Learn

Characterization

Know how your silicon ages in its real environment.

  • Per-device, per-region degradation profiles built from field measurements
  • Trends that separate normal aging from a part heading toward failure
  • Data that feeds back into placement, screening and maintenance decisions

05 · Repair

Self-healing

Degrading LUTs are repaired in place — before they corrupt a result.

  • Detection: the monitoring and characterization layers flag the failing LUT
  • Repair: the resource is restored or reconfigured without pulling the board
  • The device recovers in the field; the mission continues
