How it works

From BEL to decision

A closed loop: measure the silicon, judge its health, act on it.

Stage 1 · Measure

Monitors at every BEL

Health sensors instrument the fabric down to individual LUTs, flip-flops and routing. Timing queries return measured delay from the running silicon on demand.

Stage 2 · Judge

Characterize the degradation

Readings accumulate into per-device, per-region aging profiles. A degrading resource is identified before it fails, with the data to prove it.

Stage 3 · Act

Compensate and repair

Logic remaps to healthier resources, DVFS holds timing, and degrading LUTs are repaired in place. The device recovers; the mission continues.
