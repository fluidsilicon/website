Solutions

Highly-scaled process nodes (28nm, 16nm, 14nm, 7nm and below) have high performance and energy requirement variance, and as well as diverse reliability bounds.

Conventional static design practices assumes all transistors are identical --- with equivalent threshold voltages. In reality, there is a huge divergence on threshold voltages in Integrated Circuits (ICs), which compounds to high energy waste per chip, and at hyper-scale, a global catastrophe.

How It Works

A closed loop system on unmodified silicon that can help reconfigurable chips to monitor themselves and adapt to their unique set of conditions, and further repair themselves.
