Systems Software Engineer, HW/SW Co-Design

careers@fluidsilicon.com · Subject: "Systems Software Engineer"

About Fluid Silicon

Fluid Silicon builds the platform that lets deployed chips monitor their own health, adapt to their own silicon, and repair themselves in the field. The company was founded out of reconfigurable computing research at the University of Pennsylvania and was awarded the Penn President's Sustainability Prize. We work on commercial FPGAs, in real deployments, on problems most of the industry treats as fixed at fabrication.

The Role

You will own everything that runs above the fabric. The platform's value is a closed loop: sensors produce measurements, software turns measurements into decisions, and decisions become reconfiguration on a live device. You build the software half of that loop, from the driver that talks to the chip to the analytics that watch a fleet age.

This is a founding engineering role at the boundary of hardware and software. You will define the hardware/software contract together with the FPGA team, and the stack you build will run unattended, for years, on systems where a missed fault is a mission failure.

Responsibilities

  • Build the runtime: sensor ingestion, device health tracking, and repair orchestration
  • Write drivers and low-level interfaces to FPGA platforms over PCIe and embedded Linux
  • Design scheduling policies that apply tuning and repair in safe or idle periods
  • Build the telemetry pipeline and lifetime analytics that anticipate failures
  • Harden the stack for unattended operation on customer fleets

Minimum Qualifications

  • BS or MS in computer science, computer engineering, or equivalent experience
  • 3+ years of systems programming in C, C++, or Rust, with Python for tooling
  • Experience programming against real hardware: drivers, memory-mapped I/O, DMA, embedded Linux
  • Enough digital design literacy to read RTL and negotiate an interface with the people who write it
  • Strong judgment on reliability, observability, and failure modes in long-running systems

Preferred Qualifications

  • FPGA runtime experience such as XRT or OPAE
  • Production telemetry, fleet management, or predictive maintenance systems
  • Performance analysis and modeling of hardware/software systems
  • Contributions to open-source systems or tooling

Compensation & Benefits

  • Competitive salary and founding-level equity
  • Medical, dental, and vision coverage
  • Relocation support to Philadelphia
  • Development hardware at your desk and a lab to test against
  • Direct collaboration with the founder and technical advisors from Penn Engineering

How to Apply

Email careers@fluidsilicon.com with the subject "Systems Software Engineer". Include your resume and a link to code you have written or a short write-up of a system you built and what broke.
